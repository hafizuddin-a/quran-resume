A toolbar menu, clearer wake-up messaging.

- Clicking the toolbar icon now opens a Chrome-style menu: the chapter and verse you last stopped at, an **Open Quran** button, the once-a-day-after-sleep switch, and **Talk to the developer** (opens the madebyhafiz Instagram page).
- The wake-up prompt is shorter and says it is for waking from sleep, and that the Quran also opens whenever Chrome starts.

**To update by hand:** unzip `quran-resume-1.2.0.zip` over your folder and click reload on the extension in `chrome://extensions`.
