A welcome page, and a tidier menu.

- **Welcome page** after install: a thank-you, a picker for where to begin (surah and verse), and a reminder to close and reopen the browser. It opens once, on a fresh install only.
- The "Talk to the developer" entry now highlights together with its sign-off when you hover.

**To update by hand:** unzip `quran-resume-1.4.0.zip` over your folder and click reload on the extension in `chrome://extensions`.
