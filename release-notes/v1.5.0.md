New: carry your place to your phone. Press **Get it on your phone** in the toolbar menu, allow the connection, and scan the QR code. Your phone opens quran.com at your last verse, and a place saved on the phone is picked up by your computer. It is off until you turn it on, only your last verse and its time are sent, and **Turn off** deletes them from the server. See the README for details.

**To update by hand:** unzip `quran-resume-1.5.0.zip` over your folder and click reload on the extension in `chrome://extensions`.
