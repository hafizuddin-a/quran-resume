Fix: clicking another tab right after the browser starts no longer sends you back to the Quran tab.

- The Quran tab was brought forward again at 3.5 and 6 seconds to beat slow tab restore, which could override a click you made in that time. It is now brought forward at the start and once more about a second later, then left alone.

**To update by hand:** unzip `quran-resume-1.4.2.zip` over your folder and click reload on the extension in `chrome://extensions`.
