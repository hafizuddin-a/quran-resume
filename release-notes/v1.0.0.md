First release.

- Opens quran.com in a pinned tab at the verse you last stopped on, every time your browser starts.
- Opens the whole surah scrolled to that verse, not a single-verse view.
- Saves your place automatically as you scroll.
- Reuses an existing quran.com tab and keeps the Quran tab in front while your other tabs restore.
- Toolbar icon opens your last verse any time.
- No account, no analytics, no network requests of its own. Your last verse is stored only on your device.

**To install by hand:** download `quran-resume-1.0.0.zip` below, unzip it into a folder you'll keep, open
`chrome://extensions`, turn on Developer mode, click **Load unpacked** and choose the folder.
