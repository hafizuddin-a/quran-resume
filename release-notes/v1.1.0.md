Adds an optional once-a-day return to the Quran.

- The first time each day you return after your laptop sleeps (or you are away 15+ minutes), the Quran tab comes to the front, or reopens at your last verse if you closed it. Later returns that day are left alone.
- New options page with an on/off switch (right-click the toolbar icon, then Options).
- Adds the `idle` and `alarms` permissions. Neither shows an install warning.

**To update by hand:** unzip `quran-resume-1.1.0.zip` over your folder and click reload on the extension in `chrome://extensions`.
