Wake-up return is now exactly once a day, with a switch to turn it off.

- The first time each day your computer wakes from sleep or unlocks with the browser open, the Quran tab comes to the front (or reopens at your last verse if you closed it). Later wakes that day are left alone.
- A small prompt on the Quran page, styled like a Chrome popup, offers a switch to turn this off and a "Don't ask again" button to stop the prompt. It is on by default.
- New options page with an on/off switch (right-click the toolbar icon, then Options).
- Adds the `idle` and `alarms` permissions. Neither shows an install warning.

**To update by hand:** unzip `quran-resume-1.1.1.zip` over your folder and click reload on the extension in `chrome://extensions`.
