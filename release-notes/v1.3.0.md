More control and less friction.

- **Startup switch:** opening the Quran when Chrome starts can now be turned off in the toolbar menu. It is on by default.
- **Keyboard shortcut:** Alt+Shift+Q (Option+Shift+Q on a Mac) jumps to your Quran tab at your last verse. Change it at `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`.
- **Sync my place:** your last verse follows you to your other computers through Chrome sync, newest place wins. On by default, switch in the menu.

**To update by hand:** unzip `quran-resume-1.3.0.zip` over your folder and click reload on the extension in `chrome://extensions`.
