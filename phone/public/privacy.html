Privacy Policy

Quran Resume · Effective 10 November 2026

1. About this policy

Quran Resume is a free Chrome extension and phone web app that keeps your place in the Quran. It is made by Hafiz, an independent developer. It uses the Quran Foundation APIs (api.quran.com) for Quran text, translations, tafsir, word meanings, fonts and recitations. Quran.com and QuranReflect are services of the Quran Foundation. Quran Resume is not an official Quran Foundation, Quran.com or QuranReflect app, and it does not use Quran.com or QuranReflect accounts.

This policy covers the extension, the phone app at quran-resume.hello-hafiz.workers.dev, and the small server they share.

2. What we collect and why

There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number, and there are no analytics, ads or tracking.

Sensitive religious information

Your reading place, and confirming "I have read the Quran today", relate to religious practice, which privacy laws such as the GDPR treat as sensitive personal data needing stronger protection. This data leaves your device only with your explicit, opt-in consent, given by a clear action of your own:

  • Phone link: you press Link your phone in the extension, then confirm Link this computer? on your phone.
  • Daily reminders: you turn on Daily reminders and allow notifications.

Both are off until you choose them, neither is needed to read, and each is separate from anything else you agree to. You can turn either off at any time.

What we never do

  • We never use your data to build advertising profiles.
  • We never sell, rent, mine or repurpose your data beyond the features above.
  • We never use your data or anything you do in the app to train AI models.

4. How we protect it

  • In transit: every connection uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • At rest: our database is encrypted at rest with AES-256 by Cloudflare.
  • Access: only the developer can reach the server and its data.
  • Secrets: server keys are replaced at least once a year, and at once if we suspect one has been exposed.
  • Incidents: we start responding to a security incident within 24 hours of learning of it, and report any that involve the Quran Foundation APIs to the Quran Foundation within 24 hours, as its developer requirements ask (Security Rule 6.9).

5. Your choices and rights

  • No account to revoke: Quran Resume doesn't sign you in to Quran.com or the Quran Foundation, so there are no OAuth tokens to revoke.
  • Delete my data (phone app): Settings → Delete my data deletes your reminder record and phone link from our server, and clears everything the app kept on your phone.
  • Unlink (extension): Unlink in the toolbar menu deletes your place and pairing from our server. Removing the extension deletes everything it kept on your computer.
  • Reminders off: turning reminders off deletes your push address from our server at once.
  • Access and correction: everything we keep is shown in the app or the extension menu. Email us for a copy of what our server holds about you, or to correct it.

6. How long we keep it

Data stays only while you use the feature it's for:

  • Phone link: until you unlink. A pairing no phone joins is deleted after a day, and one with no new place for 6 months is deleted.
  • Push address: until you turn reminders off.
  • Reminder times and time zone: deleted a month after reminders are turned off.
  • Wrong link code counts: deleted within an hour.

When you ask us to delete your data, it is deleted at once, and always within 30 days. Cloudflare keeps a recovery history of our database for up to 7 days, after which deleted data is gone for good.

7. Children

Quran Resume is not directed at children under 13. There is no sign-up and we don't collect names or ages, so we can't tell who is using it. A parent who wants a child's data removed can use Delete my data, or email us.

8. International transfers

Our server runs on Cloudflare's worldwide network, so your data may be processed outside your country. For these transfers we rely on Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum, which includes the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses, to protect your data to GDPR standards. We are responsible for following the data protection laws that apply where our users are.

9. Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. Significant changes are also noted in the extension's release notes and shown in the phone app.
