Quran Resume on your phone

How to get it

This works together with the Quran Resume browser extension. On your computer, click the extension's icon, choose Get it on your phone, and scan the code with your phone's camera.

Your phone then opens quran.com at the verse where you stopped, and your place follows you between your phone and your computer.

Quran Resume is free and open source. Read the code. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with Quran.com or the Quran Foundation.
