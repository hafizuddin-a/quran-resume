Thank you for installing Quran Resume

From now on, your browser opens the Quran at the verse where you last stopped.

1 Choose where to begin

Pick a starting place. You can change it any time just by reading on quran.com, and your place is saved automatically.

2 Close and reopen Chrome

The next time your browser starts, the Quran opens by itself in a pinned tab at your place. Don't want to wait?

Tip: click the puzzle-piece icon in your toolbar and pin Quran Resume. Its menu shows your last verse and lets you change the settings.
